# 🧮 Why rare-change samples need weights

This is a **synthetic arithmetic demonstration**, with no environmental observations.

We deliberately inspect more cells in a small group than its area share warrants.
Simply counting sampled answers says category A occupies **22%**. Weighting each
group by its population share gives **13%**. This explains why “more examples in
our sample” does not automatically mean “more change across the place.”
