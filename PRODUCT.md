# Earthsignal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The public experience is for ordinary people, including high-school students
and nontechnical mayors. Its first job is to help them understand and explain
subsidence through a short visual story, animations and 3D scenes. English is
the first release language. Investigating a place is an optional next step.
Scientists, planners, responders and collaborators retain the full advanced
workspace and existing guided interpretation workflow.

## Product Purpose

Start with a public story about the ground beneath Tehran. Keep the full
place-and-question workflow, supporting evidence, conditional options and
defensible exports in the separate advanced workspace at /workspace.
The NASA Space Apps Dancing with the SARs challenge is the demonstration context.

## Operating Context

Earthsignal runs at nasa.r8volt.com with a private GitHub repository. An
authenticated gateway protects user jobs and downloads. Scientists also use
offline reproducibility tools. Optical interpretation records human judgments
about dated images, not verified ground truth or radar-date labels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve acquisition discovery, search, original studies, comparisons, masks,
analysis tools, provenance and owner-scoped results. Use the existing bounded
server and free sources; the user will acquire a stronger server later.
Observed measurements, explanations, scenarios, forecasts and annotations must
remain distinguishable. Missing evidence must not become a no-change result.
No accuracy, causality or danger claim is earned merely by displaying a map.

## Brand Commitments

Earthsignal is the existing name. User-facing language should be clear enough
for an ordinary person, including an older nontechnical mayor, while scientific
depth stays available. Public pages use a quiet dark exhibition direction.
Future-years and invented-city collapse demonstrations stay prominent, visibly
distinct from measurements. Judge the design in working scenes first.

## Evidence on Hand

The prepared radar study and historical water study have recorded identities and
measurements. `examples/optical-reference-development/` supplies frozen dated
Sentinel-2 development windows, native arrays, metadata and a verifier. The four
selected DSWx products in the recorded Lake Mead patch have no valid support.
Independent field labels, untouched final evaluation and human-task outcomes
must not be invented; see the E1–E6 scientific protocols.

## Product Principles

- Start with the user's question and explain quantities in ordinary language.
- Preserve scientific methods, units, masks, dates and evidence lineage.
- Keep uncertainty, non-observation and interpretation distinct.
- Make useful inspection and reproducibility possible within measured budgets.
- Preserve private ownership and explicit public-AI evidence boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The accepted plan targets WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, screen-reader
navigation, usable mobile controls and table equivalents for scientific charts.
First-time review collaborators need explicit instructions, visible dates,
an exact selected area and a legitimate uncertain/not-visible answer.
