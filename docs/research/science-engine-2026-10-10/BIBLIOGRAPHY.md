# Scientific bibliography and access record

This index contains the 60 non-GitHub source URLs used in REPORT.md and EXTENSIONS.md. Several URLs are different pages of the same work or documentation family. It is not a claim of 60 independently validated studies or complete reading of 60 books/papers. Repository inspections are recorded separately in repositories.json.

Primary papers, official technical documentation and author/publisher books were preferred. Bibliographic registration was independently checked for the DOI subset in citation-metadata.json. Two Crossref lookups were rate-limited; no unchanged retries were made. One incorrect supplied DOI was identified and corrected using the primary publisher and author listing. Source-specific access limits remain explicit.
