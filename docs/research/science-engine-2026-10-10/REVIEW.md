## Corrections verified in the final review

1. Experimental gauge evaluator is distinguished from live assessment prediction withholding and the separate retrospective browser experiment.
2. NOAA live APIs are distinguished from multiyear forecast-vintage archives.
3. Final-test passing-subset selection requires evaluation of the complete release policy on further untouched/prospective data.
4. SAR Gamma shape/scale, ratio orientation, equal-tail policy and ENL estimation uncertainty are explicit.
5. Coverage counterexample uses the actually reported p95: selected zero versus full-population 100.
6. Public source claims have pinned content evidence; the archived global streamflow paper-resource repository is labeled accordingly.

Additional reviewed items: climate scenario versus dated weather forecast, conditional conservation claims, corrected hydrologic uncertainty citation, normalized-difference derivatives, interval-score equation, stratified area estimator, GUNW correction status, DISP availability and limited-path disasterWatch critique.
